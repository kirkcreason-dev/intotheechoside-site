What Is Into the Echoside?

⛧ ⛧ ⛧

A fast, vicious deckbuilding card game for 2–4 players, straight from the Dark Carnival. Games run under an hour. Easy to learn, wicked to master.

⛧⛧⛧⛧

Build & Burn Karma

Every ninja starts with the same scrub deck. Play cards to pool Karma, then spend it in the Gallery on artists, Items, and Fiends from three crews. Your purchases shuffle back in — your deck becomes a weapon.

⛧⛧⛧⛧

Stomp Your Homies

This isn't solitaire. Stomp opponents' cards as they play them, sic Fiends on their Items, deny their combos — and with the expansion, bitch-slap and mirror them on their own turn.

⛧⛧⛧⛧

Race for Epics

Epic cards are worth the most Gathering Points, and when the last one is drawn, the carnival closes. Most GP wins — tiebreaker per the official rulebook: "give me my money, bitch."

Spin the Wheel of Fate

Some cards send you to the wheel — twelve fortunes on a d12, from fresh draws and free Juggalos to absolutely nothing at all. The carnival gives, and the carnival takes.

Settle it like civilized juggalos. Echoside Judge is a companion referee app for tournaments, game nights, and rules beefs — think TCG Referee, but for the Echoside.

Look up any card and get the official text, timing rules, and interaction rulings — stomps, shields, Fast effect windows, Tarot resolution order, all of it.

Match Tools

Turn timers, Karma tracking for tabletop play, first-player rolls, a Wheel of Fate roller, and round-by-round match logging.

Tournament Mode

Run brackets and swiss rounds, record results, handle disputes with a built-in penalty guide — made for DCG Con-style organized play.
